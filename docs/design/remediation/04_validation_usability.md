| Concept | Rating | Notes |

| Origin protection / concurrency cap | Good | Lab About "The problem" explains it in plain words (cap on in-flight origin requests, excess is turned away). |

| Adaptive controller / AIMD | Weak | "AIMD" is never expanded in the UI text I saw; explained only as "a limit that moves between a minimum and a maximum". |
| Arms, paired runs, shed, mechanism check, falsifiers, H1 to H6 | Weak | Statistical and protocol jargon without inline glossary. |
| Intelligence / cluster | Adequate | Clearly "not available", wording inconsistent (U-09). |

Product coherence: Lab, Overview and Simulation share one visual language, badge system and honesty stance, and cross-link well. Gaps: Overview does not orient a newcomer; "Reports mode: live" and Lab's "development" mode are two unrelated mode notions; the Simulation page "retired demo" notice references history a newcomer does not have.

Jargon that blocked or slowed me: "units" (progress), "arm", "paired", "shed", "mechanism check", "H-B1 falsified", "practically null", "BCa", "T1/T2", "AIMD", "canary mode" (in the intelligence note).

## What an AI walkthrough cannot establish
Real human confusion or misreadings (I read all text and resolved jargon by context; people skim); time-on-task and error rates; learnability over time; trust calibration (do users over-trust REAL or VALID?); accessibility needs (screen reader, keyboard-only, low vision, motor, cognitive load); pointer and touch ergonomics (clicks were programmatic); real mobile devices; long-session behaviour. Task wording leaked domain terms.

## Recommended human usability study
- 5 to 8 participants: 3 to 4 target (backend/SRE/performance engineers), 2 to 3 adjacent (frontend or data engineers with no project exposure), include at least one screen-reader or keyboard-only user and one phone user. Moderated, think-aloud, 45 to 60 min, no briefing on project terms.
- Tasks: T1 to T8 as above, rephrased without product vocabulary (for example "start a short test against a local gateway" rather than "Scenario B"). Fresh gateway on the default URL for T6 (tests U-02).
- Success measures: task completion without help; steps and time per task; first-click correctness; number of hesitations and help requests; comprehension probes after T1, T4, T5 (can they state, in their own words, what REAL/SIMULATED/DERIVED and VALID/EXPLORATORY mean; did the adaptive controller beat static; was anything measured on the Simulation page); post-task confidence and trust ratings; SUS and a short custom trust-calibration item. Pre-register pass thresholds (for example at least 80% correct on the T4 and T5 comprehension probes, no participant concluding that a cancelled run falsified a hypothesis).
- Log severity per participant issue and compare against U-01 to U-14.
