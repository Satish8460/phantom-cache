## 1. Short answer

The shortfall is mostly **not** one agent failing; it is a chain of process gaps, several of them mine (the lead, i.e. Claude in the main session):

1. The owner's design requirement was expressed only as **adjectives and principles** ("premium", "clarity, restraint, hierarchy, precision, purposeful motion"), never as measurable acceptance criteria, and the lead's briefs **repeated the adjectives** without adding criteria (T:1892, T:4803).
2. The one control that would have caught this, **owner/design approval of artifacts before coding** (AC8-4, M8-07 in `phase_8_execution_package.md:88,113`), was **waived by the lead** on its own authority (`progress_ledger.md:100`) and the wireframes, prototypes, state gallery and chart specs it required were **never produced** (verified absent).
3. **Verification measured conformance, not quality.** 200+ node tests are static/server-render scans ("Visual quality is not tested here", `ui/test/lab.render.test.mjs:2`), and the three browser rounds measured overflow, targets, contrast and console errors; agents opened about a dozen of ~330 screenshots and judged "hierarchy and contrast looked fine" (`browser_verification/20261030T045605Z/report.md:33`).
4. The named Apple design resource was **evaluated but forbidden to the implementer** ("Do not adopt external skills", T:1892) and the adoption decision O8-1 was left open for two days; the skill was first invoked at 2026-10-30T07:01Z (T:6086), after the work was built.
5. **Competing priorities**: the owner, in the same messages, ranked honesty/truthfulness above polish ("not merely look more polished", T:1744; "attractive UI is not product quality", T:3052), earlier told the team not to expand frontend scope (AG2:184, AG2:4780), and M8 was sequenced last behind M1 to M7 and the sealed research runs. The legacy `SimDemo.jsx` was kept intact and exempted from the design checks.

## 5. Ranked root causes (five most important)

1. **No measurable design acceptance criteria and no visual design artifacts before coding** (D3, D4): the owner's adjectives were passed through the lead's briefs unchanged; the wireframe/prototype/gallery gate was waived (`progress_ledger.md:100`).
2. **Verification measured conformance instead of quality** (D9, D10): static tests and mechanical browser checks, ~12 of ~330 screenshots viewed, no rubric, no independent human design review.
3. **The Apple design resource was evaluated but kept away from the implementer** (D1, D7): O8-1 stayed open; the brief forbade adoption; interaction/motion standards never reached the code.
4. **Priority conflicts left unresolved** (D12, D13): honesty ranked above polish, frontend scope earlier restricted, M8 sequenced last; nobody declared design quality a gate with an owner.
5. **Legacy `SimDemo.jsx` retained and exempted from the design system and its tests** (D8): a different product inside the same shell, with sub-12 px text contradicting a stated principle.

## 6. Process changes: acceptance criteria and review gates for future UI briefs

**Brief template (every UI brief must contain):**
1. *Reference set:* 3-5 named reference screens or the pinned skill sections (S1-S17) the work must cite; builder lists which section drove each decision.
2. *Measurable criteria* (examples; owner may adjust): at least 5 distinct type tiers with a documented metric tier (>= 2x body); at most 2 banner/status rows above the primary task at 1280x800; one elevation scale with each level used at least once and shown in a gallery; every interactive control has a pointer-down state and a 100-200 ms transition (cite skill section); no screen with > 1 viewport of unbroken prose before the primary action; forms: grouped fields, errors only after interaction; every screen passes a "squint test" (primary action identifiable in 3 s) recorded by a reviewer.
3. *Required artifacts before code:* low-fi wireframe, a state x provenance gallery rendered from fixtures, and a before/after screenshot set; owner or named reviewer signs off (a recorded decision, not a lead autonomy clause).
4. *No legacy exemptions:* every file under `ui/src` is subject to the same guards; an exemption needs a dated owner decision and an expiry.
5. *Definition of done:* the builder renders the screens (or a separate step does) and attaches screenshots at 390/820/1280 in light and dark.

**Review gates:**
- **G-D1 design approval** (artifacts, before coding), by owner or designated human.
- **G-D2 screenshot review with rubric** (hierarchy, spacing, density, consistency, state coverage, motion), reviewer must open every screen at 3 widths x 2 themes (not a sample) and score each against the criteria; the reviewer is not the author.
- **G-D3 independent human design review** (owner's "commission an independent human design review", T:5951), plus assistive-technology pass.
- **Status labels:** M8 stays "IMPLEMENTED, DESIGN REVIEW NOT DONE" until G-D2/G-D3 pass; green tests do not change it.
- **Test-suite honesty:** tests that assert style conformance must say so; add a screenshot baseline or visual-regression decision to the owner list (dev-dependency, O8-3).
