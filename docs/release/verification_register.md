## Independence classes used
- **human**: a person other than the author checked retained evidence or code. **None is recorded in the repository.**
- **AI second look**: a separate AI agent or the lead re-checked. Labelled so; it is not human independent verification.
- **lead re-run**: the lead repeated a command (an AI in the same project; closer to a reproduction than to independent review).
- **agent-reported**: the author agent's own report, not re-checked.
- **re-run by DOCS**: I ran the command during this refresh.
Stale = the tree or conditions changed after the check, or the evidence is no longer retained.
