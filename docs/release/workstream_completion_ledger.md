## Vocabulary
- **Status class:** COMPLETE (finished and recorded as such in its own record), IMPLEMENTED (exists, tested or not), TESTED (a named automated check passed; says nothing about correctness), IN PROGRESS, BLOCKED, PENDING (approved or queued, not started), NOT STARTED, DEFERRED, SUPERSEDED. A row may carry a qualifier in the status cell.
- **Independence class:** *human* (a person other than the author checked retained evidence or code; none is recorded anywhere in the repository), *AI second look* (a separate AI agent or the lead re-checked; useful, not human independent verification), *agent-reported* (only the author agent's own report), *none*. INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED requires a reviewer other than the author; no row below reaches it with a human reviewer.
- "Formal acceptance" by the owner is **Not recorded** for every milestone; this is an evidence gap, not proof that none was given.
