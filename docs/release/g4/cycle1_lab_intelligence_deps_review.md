**F13 Medium CONFIRMED. Shadow evaluator confidence interval assumes independent arms and independent requests (shadow.rs:452-465).**

Direction of the bias is mixed (pairing makes the CI too wide, dependence makes it too narrow), so the reported 95% has no stated coverage. Fix: paired difference with block bootstrap or per-key clustering and a warm-up discard. Test: stream with identical policies must give delta 0 and `is_statistically_significant=false` for many seeds (A/A false-positive rate under 5%). Frozen file.
