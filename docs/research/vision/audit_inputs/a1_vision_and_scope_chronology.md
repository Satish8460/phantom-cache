### 1.3 INFERENCE (separated from the facts above)

1. **Distributed was in the original name and architecture, but the Oct 11 "distributed" was an in-process simulation.** The committed README labels the cluster "Modeled / Simulated" and the whole `src/dist/` was 117 lines (48+69). There was no network protocol, no node-to-node cache traffic and no cache-backed HTTP path. Confidence: high (git:456bb25 stat and README:38).
2. **"Multi-node as a core differentiator" is plausible but not recoverable from the repo.** Evidence for: the word "distributed" in the title/README, a dedicated WP3, rendezvous routing, the owner's own P2 priority, the owner commissioning Phase 3 and 4 as distributed work, and the owner's 3-device demo ambition. Evidence against or ambiguous: the owner's own 2026-10-28 question (CC:427) about whether the intent was local-cache-plus-invalidation vs coordinated reads; the handoff non-goal "Not a Distributed Database" (`docs/handoff/01_PROJECT_MASTER_CONTEXT.md:27`); no document before 2026-10-30 calls multi-node a USP. Verdict: **multi-node was a stated, recurring ambition and the named architecture, but the original differentiator rationale, node-coordination expectations and acceptance criteria are not documented in the repo; they probably lived in the missing ChatGPT conversation (GAP).**
3. **Original expectations around coordination/invalidation/fault tolerance** exist in recoverable form only as WP3 (node state machine, HRW primary + replicas 2 and 3, simulated replication, `NODE_FAILURE`) and later as the owner's P2 text and AG's Phase 3/4 designs. "Distributed workload intelligence", "coordinated pre-warming" and similar items in the audit request have **no recoverable source before the Oct 30 vision docs** (`docs/research/vision/00_consolidated_vision_and_feasibility_report.md:99` R15: cluster-wide warming "blocked on durable invalidation; warming stays node-local"). GAP: they may have been in the ChatGPT conversation.
