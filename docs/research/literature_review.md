|---|---|---|---|

**Assessment (agent-reported, consistent with my reading of the code):** per-key fencing, primary-directed invalidation with async broadcast, and bounded replay with a flush fallback each re-derive known techniques (Memcache leases and replay, TAO versions, Redis placeholder and flush). A publishable contribution would need what is missing: a formal model or proof (MuCache style), a *measured* staleness bound under injected loss/reordering/partition (PBS or Polaris style), a head-to-head comparison with lease and versioned approaches, and an analysis of flush-induced metastability. Without these it is a well-engineered re-derivation. The agent did not search CDN purge protocols (Fastly, Cloudflare, Akamai), so "no prior art" cannot be claimed.

### 4.1 What is already solved (agent assessment, consistent with the pages I re-opened)
Stampede suppression (coalescing, leases, XFetch); hiding refresh latency and surviving hard failure (SWR/SIE, static); service-level overload control; the latency model of coalesced misses (delayed hits); backend-latency-driven cache *allocation* (RobinHood); and the problem statement itself (metastable failures).

### 4.2 What appears unsolved — with the evidence and its limits
No peer-reviewed cache found that **adapts stale-serving windows, refresh behavior or miss admission from measured origin latency/error rate under an explicit staleness/origin-load contract**, nor one that jointly treats coalescing, early refresh and stale serving under a time-varying origin service rate. Evidence: absence in general web searches and the pages opened. **Limits:** ACM DL was inaccessible; no systematic search of OSDI/NSDI/SoCC/EuroSys 2023–2026 metastability work was done. **Absence of evidence, not proof.** This must be re-checked before any novelty claim.

### 4.3 Smallest defensible novelty (provisional)
A cache-side controller under an explicit contract (staleness cap, origin-QPS cap) that uses measured origin signals to set refresh/stale/admission policy, evaluated against tuned static baselines (nginx lock + use_stale, Varnish grace, XFetch, singleflight, Netflix/Breakwater-style limiter) on **metastable-style scenarios** (cold restart, slow origin, retry storm), and showing a recovery result those baselines do not achieve. Do not claim novelty for coalescing, SWR/SIE, early expiration, adaptive concurrency, or delayed-hit analysis; do not claim "first cache to react to origin health" (Varnish `std.healthy`, RobinHood).

### Round-2 conclusion (provisional)
- Policy selection, shadow evaluation and fallback all exist; **do not** make them the thesis.
- The cold-restart metastable scenario has a known *model-level* remedy (Farahbakhsh App. B). Our evaluation must include those remedies as baselines.
- Across everything checked, **no work or shipping system was found that couples measured origin latency/error signals to stale-serving, refresh and miss admission in a cache under explicit staleness and origin-load caps.** The strongest remaining exposures: unchecked vendor docs, the unread patent, the unread full text of Rajomon/TopFull/Protego, and the pending core origin-aware slice.
- Biggest "novelty fails" argument a reviewer will make: *Varnish health-conditional grace + DynamoDB constant-work refresh + request coalescing, tuned by a controller.* Defence = a head-to-head against exactly those tuned baselines, with continuous multi-signal health, the explicit staleness and origin-load caps, and metastable-style evaluation as differentiators. Argue explicitly against Mao et al. (HotNets'24) and RobinHood.
- The defensible claim shape, if it holds: *a measured, contract-bounded integration evaluated against tuned static and adjacent-field baselines (Envoy-style limiter, Breakwater-style admission, retry budgets, XFetch, no-timeout tagged fills)* — not "first to react to origin health".

## 5. Implications
1. **Candidate D (safe policy selection) is more crowded than I judged.** SCION and LAH/S4-FIFO (2026), Darwin, CACHEUS and ACME all occupy it. Treat as baseline/ablation, not the thesis. Candidate E (cost-aware eviction) is classical (GDS/GDSF, Hyperbolic, LHD).
2. **Candidate F (invalidation)** is mostly re-derivation unless extended with a formal model and a measured staleness bound.
3. **Candidate A/B (origin-aware miss management) survives the first check**, narrowly: the literature supports the motivation (metastable failures) and every component separately, but I found no integrated, feedback-driven, contract-bounded cache controller. RobinHood and Varnish `std.healthy` are the nearest and bound the novelty claim. **The claim must be re-verified with a systematic 2023–2026 search before we commit.**
4. **Candidate C (delayed-hit-aware coalescing)** is an active, recent area (SIGCOMM'20 → NSDI'26); not a safe thesis.
5. The existing PhantomCache "SIEVE" and "S3-FIFO" must be reimplemented faithfully before any comparison; otherwise comparisons against the real algorithms are invalid.
